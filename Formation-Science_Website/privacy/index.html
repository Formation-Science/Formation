Privacy Policy

Last updated 18 September 2026 · Example document

Note: This is a plain-language starting draft for Formation Science LLC. Have it reviewed by an attorney before relying on it — especially before submitting a carrier (A2P 10DLC) messaging campaign, which requires specific SMS-consent language, and before your first real cold-outreach campaign, since consent requirements differ for people who contact you first versus people you contact first.

Formation Science LLC ("Formation Science," "we," "us") operates formationscience.com and provides business-operation automation services to client businesses. This policy explains what we collect through this website and how we handle it.

1. Information we collect

  • Information you give us. When you submit the contact form or email, call, or text us, we receive your name, business name, phone number, email address, number of field technicians, and whatever you write about your operations.
  • Your consent to be contacted. The contact form requires you to check a box agreeing to be called or texted before it can be submitted. We keep a record of that consent — the exact wording you agreed to and when — alongside your submission.
  • Basic technical data. Standard server logs (IP address, browser type, pages viewed, timestamps) collected by our hosting provider for security and reliability.
  • We do not use advertising trackers or sell data. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18.

2. How we use it

  • To respond to your inquiry and schedule a walkthrough call.
  • To prepare a proposal and, if you become a client, to deliver and support your automation system.
  • To keep the website secure and functioning.

We rely on your request for contact (and, if you engage us, our contract with you) as the basis for this processing.

3. Messaging consent

By checking the consent box on our contact form, you agree that Formation Science may call or text you at the number you provided about your inquiry, including by automated means. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out of texts at any time; reply HELP for help. We do not sell or share phone numbers or message content with third parties for their marketing. If you contact us by phone or text without submitting the form, replying to us is treated as consent to continue that conversation the same way.

4. How we share information

We don't sell your information. We share it only with the specific service providers that run this website and our internal operations, each of which processes it on our instructions and only for that purpose:

  • Cloudflare — hosts this website and protects it from abuse.
  • Google (Google Forms / Workspace) — receives and stores your form submission for our review.

We may also disclose information if required by law. For client engagements, any handling of your customers' data is governed by the separate services agreement signed with that client.

5. Retention

Inquiry messages are kept for up to 24 months, then deleted, unless you become a client (in which case records are kept for the life of the engagement plus any period required for tax and legal purposes).

6. Your choices

You can ask us to access, correct, or delete the information you submitted by emailing hello@formationscience.com. You can opt out of texts by replying STOP and out of emails using the unsubscribe link or by replying to any message.

7. Security

We use dedicated, access-controlled infrastructure and encryption in transit. No method of transmission or storage is perfectly secure, but we do not cut corners on protecting what you send us.

8. Analytics

We don't currently use tracking cookies or behavioral analytics on this site. If we add privacy-focused analytics or a session-recording tool in the future, we'll update this section to name the specific tool and describe what it collects before turning it on.

9. Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date above and post the new version here.
