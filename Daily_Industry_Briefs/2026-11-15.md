Additive manufacturing has stopped being a design toy and is now standard factory production equipment. Machine shops are replacing multi-part metal assemblies with single printed components to kill supply chain delays. For your automation agency, every physical production step shifting digital creates an integration bottleneck you can monetize. If you connect CAD databases directly to additive queues via custom webhooks, you cut quoting cycles from days to minutes. The play here is packaging automated file validation and order intake for industrial machine shops.

NestWorks launched the C500 with an 800-watt spindle, automated tool changer, and vision-assisted setup.
